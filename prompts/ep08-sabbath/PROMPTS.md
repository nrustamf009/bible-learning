## Bright cartoon (kids 6–11)

### p1 · verses 23

```text
STYLE: bright friendly cartoon illustration for children, modern animated picture-book style, rounded shapes, big expressive eyes, thick clean dark outlines, flat saturated colors with soft cel shading, warm and joyful mood, simple uncluttered background, never scary or gory, no text, no letters, no speech bubbles.

SCENE (wide, golden): Jesus and the disciples walking through a golden wheat field, the disciples picking heads of grain and eating them.

SETTING: a field of ripe golden barley in Galilee in spring, a footpath through it, low stone boundary walls; Capernaum, a fishing town on the north shore, houses of black basalt stone with flat roofs of beams and clay, a white limestone synagogue.

CHARACTERS (keep exactly the same look in every image):
- Jesus of Nazareth, a Jewish man about 30 years old, olive-brown skin, dark brown wavy shoulder-length hair parted in the middle, short full dark beard, warm dark brown eyes, kind steady expression; cream undyed linen tunic to the ankles, deep blue woolen outer mantle with small blue-and-white tassels at the corners, leather sandals; soft round face, gentle smile, always the calmest person in the scene
- Simon Peter, a burly Galilean fisherman about 40, very broad shoulders, sun-tanned skin, short curly black hair with grey at the temples, thick bushy grey-black beard, big expressive eyebrows; rough brown knee-length tunic with sleeves rolled up, rope belt, bare forearms; big round body, huge eyebrows that show every emotion
- John son of Zebedee, the youngest disciple, about 18, slim, olive skin, curly black hair, no beard (only light fuzz), large bright eyes, open curious face; sky-blue tunic, simple belt; looks like a big kid, very expressive
- Andrew, Peter's younger brother, about 35, lean and wiry, tanned skin, long dark hair tied back at the neck, scruffy short beard, thoughtful eyes; faded sage-green tunic, rope belt; tall and thin, small ponytail

COMPOSITION: wide 3:2 frame; keep the top quarter of the image calm and uncluttered (speech bubbles will be added later).

AVOID: text, letters, watermark, speech bubbles, photorealism, gore, horror, creepy faces, extra fingers, European medieval clothing, modern objects.
```
